FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What's the difference between fixed and month-to-month?

A fixed-rate plan locks in your energy charge for the whole term, so your price per kWh won't change. Month-to-month plans have no contract and no early termination fee, but the rate can change with the market.

What is an Electricity Facts Label (EFL)?

Every Texas retail electricity plan has an EFL that lists the average price at 500, 1,000 and 2,000 kWh, the base charge, TDU charges, term length and renewable content. It's the best way to compare apples to apples.

Will my power be interrupted when I switch?

Nope. Your local utility still delivers your power through the same poles and wires. The only thing that changes is who sends your bill — and how much you pay.

What happens when my plan expires?

We'll reach out before your contract ends with renewal options. If you don't pick a new plan, you'll roll onto a month-to-month plan with no cancellation fee.

Do I need a deposit?

It depends on your credit history. Many customers qualify for no deposit, and we offer deposit alternatives like a letter of credit from a previous provider.
